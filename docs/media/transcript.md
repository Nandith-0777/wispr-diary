# Wispr Diary — promo narration

Your day deserves more than a forgotten note. Meet Wispr Diary.

Make a book of your own. Give it a title, choose a cover, and make a little space for your day.

A short welcome shows you where everything lives. Skip it anytime, or come back to the tour from the Book menu.

Tell the story of your day, or type it. Your words belong on the page, with more room whenever you need it.

Diary, add note: remember the little things. Keep a thought on a sticky note, and move it around by its grip.

Bring the day back with a photograph, a video, or a voice recording. Keep each memory right beside your words.

Choose lined or blank paper, and a page color you love. Contents brings you back to any part of your story.

Your books stay in your account. Save unfinished thoughts in Ideas for Later, until they find their page.

Wispr Diary. Less typing. More living.
